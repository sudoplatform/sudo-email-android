setAllowCallerManagedKeys

fun setAllowCallerManagedKeys(allowCallerManagedKeys: Boolean): <Error class: unknown class>

Provide whether the app may supply the key pair an email address or email alias is provisioned with, as the keyId of ProvisionEmailAddressInput or ProvisionEmailAliasInput (optional input). Defaults to true.

When true, the app is responsible for its key pairs, so deprovisioning never removes one: key pairs stay on the device until reset, including those the client generated.

When false, the client manages every key pair itself: provisioning with a keyId throws EmailAddressException.InvalidArgumentException or EmailAliasException.InvalidArgumentException, and deprovisioning an email address removes its key pairs from the device once no other email address or email alias uses them; see deprovisionEmailAddress. Deprovisioning removes nothing while setEnforceSingletonKeyPair is true.
